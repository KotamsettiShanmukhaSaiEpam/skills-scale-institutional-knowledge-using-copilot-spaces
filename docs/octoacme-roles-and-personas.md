# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance (QA) Lead

### Role Summary
The QA Lead owns the quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure features meet quality standards before release and collaborate with developers and product managers on testability and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test strategy and QA approach for each project phase
- Create and execute comprehensive test plans for each feature
- Validate acceptance criteria before marking work as done
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Track defects, quality metrics, and trend analysis
- Collaborate on test automation and CI/CD integration
- Conduct acceptance testing with stakeholders and product managers

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Reviews testability of code, provides feedback on test coverage, collaborates on bug fixes
- **With Product Managers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria, validates feature completeness, communicates quality trade-offs
- **With Technical Lead**: Ensures test architecture aligns with system design, identifies technical testing challenges
- **With Project Manager**: Reports quality status, escalates blockers, tracks defect metrics

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and reduce production defects
- Provide fast, actionable feedback to developers
- Enable continuous delivery with confidence
- Reduce rework and post-release issues

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and QA approach reviews
- Acceptance criteria walkthroughs with developers and product managers
- Defect triage and prioritization meetings
- Quality metrics and trend reports
- Test execution and verification updates

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
The Technical Lead provides architectural guidance, design reviews, and ensures technical decisions align with project goals, scalability, and maintainability. They mentor the development team and guide technology choices.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture proposals
- Conduct code reviews for architectural alignment and best practices
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Guide technology choices and trade-offs
- Ensure adherence to coding standards and architectural patterns
- Mentor developers on best practices and design principles
- Maintain technical documentation and architectural decision records (ADRs)
- Collaborate on performance optimization and scalability planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides technical mentorship, reviews code, guides problem-solving
- **With Product Managers**: Discusses technical feasibility, trade-offs, and implementation approaches
- **With QA Lead**: Reviews test architecture, identifies edge cases and testing challenges
- **With Project Manager**: Flags technical risks, estimates effort, manages technical dependencies

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable faster feature delivery through good architecture
- Build team technical competency

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Risk assessments and technical trade-off discussions
- Documentation and architectural decision records (ADRs)
- Technical spike discussions and prototyping

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
The UX/Design Lead owns user experience strategy, interaction design, and visual design. They ensure design decisions are documented, validated with users, and reviewed by stakeholders and engineers for feasibility.

### Responsibilities
- Define UX strategy and user research approach
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Review and approve design implementations
- Ensure consistency across product interfaces and user flows
- Identify and resolve design risks early
- Collaborate with developers on design feasibility and implementation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Reviews implementation against design specs, provides design guidance for edge cases
- **With Product Managers**: Aligns design with product strategy, validates user experience impact
- **With Technical Lead**: Discusses feasibility of design approaches, optimizes performance impact
- **With QA Lead**: Collaborates on acceptance criteria for design quality, verifies visual/interaction correctness

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centric product experiences
- Reduce support burden through good design
- Enable consistent brand and user experience
- Validate assumptions through user feedback

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specifications and implementation guides
- Usability testing results and recommendations
- Design pattern documentation

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator removes blockers, facilitates agile ceremonies, and ensures the team follows agreed-upon processes. They enable team productivity and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Monitor sprint progress and flag risks
- Maintain project board and backlog hygiene
- Drive continuous improvement through retrospectives
- Shield the team from external distractions and scope creep

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With All Roles**: Facilitates communication, removes blockers, ensures process adherence
- **With Project Manager**: Coordinates schedule, escalates blockers, tracks delivery metrics
- **With Product Manager**: Manages backlog prioritization, ensures clarity on acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and productivity
- Reduce cycle time and bottlenecks
- Foster continuous improvement culture
- Enable team autonomy and self-organization

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremony facilitation and meeting notes
- Daily blocker status and resolution tracking
- Retrospective action items and follow-up
- Team health and velocity metrics

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
The Security/Compliance Officer ensures security and compliance requirements are integrated throughout the project lifecycle. They conduct reviews, identify risks, and ensure adherence to security standards and regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements early in planning
- Conduct security reviews of designs, architecture, and code
- Identify and assess security risks and vulnerabilities
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and corporate standards
- Track security issues and remediation plans
- Provide security training and best practices guidance
- Participate in incident response and post-incident reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides security guidance, reviews code for vulnerabilities
- **With Technical Lead**: Reviews architecture for security and compliance implications
- **With Product Manager**: Clarifies compliance requirements, discusses trade-offs
- **With Project Manager**: Escalates security risks, tracks remediation, updates risk register

### Goals
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and compliance violations
- Embed security throughout the development lifecycle
- Minimize security-related incidents and costs
- Maintain regulatory compliance and audit readiness

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews and specifications
- Code and design security reviews
- Risk assessments and vulnerability reports
- Security training and awareness sessions
- Compliance and audit documentation

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
The DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer manages deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and operational readiness. They ensure systems are scalable, reliable, and secure throughout the project lifecycle and in production.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and CI/CD processes
- Manage infrastructure, databases, and cloud environments
- Ensure system scalability, reliability, and disaster recovery
- Monitor production systems and set up observability
- Optimize costs and resource utilization
- Collaborate on performance testing and load planning
- Support incident response and postmortems

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides deployment guidance, supports local development setup
- **With Technical Lead**: Aligns infrastructure design with architecture, discusses operational requirements
- **With QA Lead**: Supports test environment setup, enables performance and load testing
- **With Project Manager**: Communicates deployment readiness, infrastructure risks, and timelines

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments
- Minimize downtime and operational issues
- Optimize cost and resource efficiency
- Provide observability and incident response capability

### Typical Communication
- Deployment pipeline and infrastructure reviews
- Performance and load test results
- Operational metrics and monitoring dashboards
- Incident reports and postmortems
- Infrastructure planning and capacity discussions

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and decision-making authority. They ensure projects align with business objectives and receive regular updates on progress and risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Provide strategic direction and context
- Approve project charters and key decisions
- Allocate resources and budget
- Escalate issues impacting business outcomes
- Communicate project value to broader organization
- Provide feedback and validation during reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Manager**: Aligns product strategy with business goals, reviews progress
- **With Project Manager**: Receives status updates, approves decisions, escalates issues
- **With Development Team**: Provides business context, participates in reviews and demos

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain alignment with organizational strategy
- Manage risk and decision-making effectively
- Communicate and celebrate project success

### Typical Communication
- Project charter and approval reviews
- Weekly or milestone-based status updates
- Executive summaries and dashboards
- Decision gates and governance meetings
- Demo reviews and feedback sessions

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Personas interact across multiple workflows defined in other OctoAcme process documents (e.g., initiation, planning, execution, release).
- Understanding role interactions helps teams anticipate communication needs and manage dependencies effectively.
